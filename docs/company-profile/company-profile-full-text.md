### Speaker notes

Steve Jobs told a story about carpentry: when you build a chest of drawers, you do not use plywood on the back, even though it faces the wall and nobody will ever see it. You know it is there. That is how we work at Mawkish. A typical enterprise deal has several parties: the software principal such as SAP, Salesforce or AWS, a specialist implementation partner, and our own team. Most problems in a project hide in the gaps between those parties and in the details nobody looks at. Our role is to own those gaps. From presales, through implementation, to support after go-live, we check every handover, hold every party to the agreed quality, and stay accountable for the outcome. Clients see the front of the cabinet. We obsess over the back.
