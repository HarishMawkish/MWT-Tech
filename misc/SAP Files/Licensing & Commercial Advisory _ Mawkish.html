How SAP licenses Cloud ERP

Four levels of user, each one including the level below.

You buy SAP Cloud ERP as a package, mainly Finance or Supply Chain, with a mix of user types. Each user type includes everything below it, so a Finance Base user can also do everything an Operational or Self-Service user can.

The skill is in giving each person the lowest level that covers their actual work. Most companies have far fewer full-access users than they assume.

Premium

Finance Premium · Supply Chain Premium

Everything in Base, plus wider scope such as projects, subscription billing, sustainability and compliance, and premium AI.

Base

Finance Base · Supply Chain Base · Combo Base

Full finance or full supply chain processes. Combo Base covers both for cross-functional power users.

Operational

Day-to-day transaction users

Sales orders, purchasing, deliveries, service and project tasks.

Self-Service

Everyone else

Approvals, requests, timesheets, confirmations and display.

The detail most people miss

Your security roles decide your bill.

In SAP Cloud ERP, each person's user type is set by the highest-level app they can access. Give an accounts payable clerk one advanced finance app "just in case", and they're licensed as Finance Base instead of Operational, for every month of the contract. That's why licence sizing and role design have to be done together.

SAP's standard roles are designed for demos and give people more access than they need. They should never go to production unchanged, for security reasons as well as cost.

Beyond the user count

Six things that change the total.

The user mix is the biggest number, but it isn't the only one. These are the items we check on every quote, because they're the ones that surprise people in year two.

Premium AI

Premium packages come with a required purchase of SAP AI Units, which pay for AI agents and assistants. Budget for it from the start.

Integrations

Documents created in SAP by other systems, such as an e-commerce site, count against your digital access allowance.

Data growth

System memory grows with users. High transaction volumes may need extra memory blocks, or archiving.

Add-ons

Some capabilities, such as advanced planning, group consolidation or extra industry scope, are licensed separately. We confirm which ones you need.

Growth and phases

If you roll out in phases or are adding entities, we plan when users join, so you don't pay for users before they go live.

Existing contracts

Customers on older GROW (FUE-based) contracts, or with SAP products like Concur, have specific rules on what they're entitled to.

Questions we hear

Straight answers.

What happened to FUEs?

For new contracts, SAP replaced Full Use Equivalents with named user types priced per user per month in 2025. Existing GROW customers on FUE contracts can stay on them. Whether to move to the new model is a case-by-case decision, and we can model both for you.

What are SAP AI Units?

They're the currency for SAP's premium AI: agents and assistants that carry out work for you. Basic AI, including Joule and embedded AI features, is included with every user. Premium packages come with a required purchase of AI Units.

What is digital access?

When another system creates documents in SAP, such as sales orders from a web shop or invoices from a portal, those documents are counted. Finance and Supply Chain users come with a yearly allowance that covers most mid-sized companies. We size it from your integration list.

Can we change our user mix later?

You can add users and user types during the contract as you grow. Reducing them usually has to wait until renewal, which is why getting the starting mix right matters.

Does this apply to RISE with SAP too?

SAP Cloud ERP Private has its own packages, but the same principle applies: licence by role, check entitlements, and plan for growth. We advise on both.

You're an SAP partner. Is your advice independent?

We're an SAP partner, and we say so. Our interest is a customer who stays with us for years, and over-selling licences on day one is the fastest way to lose that customer. Every number we give you is backed by your own roles and SAP's published user-type definitions.
