Planning

One plan, everyone working in it.

Each cost centre manager enters their budget in a form with last year's actuals beside it. Head office sets the drivers, such as exchange rates, salary increases and inflation, and the numbers update for everyone. Finance sees progress in real time and approves each submission.

The approved plan goes back to SAP Cloud ERP, so your reports there compare actual against plan without anyone copying numbers. We start from SAP's ready-made planning content for finance and adapt it to your structure.

AI in analytics

Ask why, and get an answer.

When a number moves, SAP Analytics Cloud can tell you what drove it: which region, product or customer made the difference. Managers can ask questions in plain English and get a chart back. Predictive forecasting uses your own history to suggest next quarter's numbers as a starting point for the plan.

With Joule, SAP's AI assistant, your teams can ask questions across analytics and SAP Cloud ERP, and SAP's AI can now summarise a whole dashboard in a few sentences.

Questions we hear

Straight answers.

Is SAP Analytics Cloud included in SAP Cloud ERP?

SAP Cloud ERP includes its own reporting and can show some SAP Analytics Cloud content inside the ERP. Full dashboards and planning need a separate SAP Analytics Cloud subscription. We tell you what you need for your use.

Can we keep using Excel?

Yes. SAP Analytics Cloud has an Excel add-in, so finance teams can work with live, governed data in Excel when they prefer it.

Is our data copied out of SAP?

For dashboards, SAP Analytics Cloud can read data live from SAP Cloud ERP without copying it. Planning works on its own copy of the data it needs, which is kept in step with SAP.

How long does a first budget take to set up?

It depends on your structure and approval steps. We plan it so you're ready before your budget cycle starts, starting from SAP's ready-made finance planning content.

Can managers use it on their phones?

Yes. Dashboards work on phones and tablets through SAP's mobile app, with the same access rules as on the desktop.
