RISE with SAP

Move your SAP ERP to the cloud without starting over.

RISE with SAP takes the SAP system your business already runs on and moves it to SAP Cloud ERP Private, on your own private cloud instance. You keep your history, your processes and the custom work that sets you apart. Mawkish plans the move, runs the conversion, and supports you afterwards.

About the names. RISE with SAP is the offering. The software inside it is SAP Cloud ERP Private, also known as SAP S/4HANA Cloud Private Edition.

What you get

Your SAP system, run by SAP, on the cloud you choose.

RISE brings the software, the infrastructure and the technical operations together under one SAP contract. SAP looks after the servers, database and backups. You and Mawkish focus on the business processes that run on them.

Unlike GROW with SAP, you get your own private instance. That means room for deeper customisation, all of SAP's industry solutions, and upgrades on your own schedule.

Why now

The clock on SAP ECC is running.

Mainstream maintenance for SAP ECC ends in 2027. Extended maintenance runs to 2030 at extra cost. A conversion takes most companies 12 to 24 months once you include planning, so the companies starting now are the ones that finish on their own terms.

More than a lift and shift

Come out with a cleaner system, ready for AI.

Moving to the cloud with all your old workarounds only moves the problem. In most ECC systems, more than half the custom code is never used. We use the move to retire what's dead, replace what the standard now does, and move the rest to upgrade-safe extensions.

A clean core is also what makes SAP's AI useful. Joule and AI agents work best on standard processes and clean data. A cleaner system pays off from day one.

Questions we hear

Straight answers.

Is RISE with SAP just hosting?

No. Hosting moves your servers somewhere else. RISE moves you to SAP Cloud ERP Private, with infrastructure, technical operations and tools under one SAP contract. You also move from ECC to SAP S/4HANA along the way.

Can we keep our custom code?

Yes. Private Edition allows classic custom code, unlike GROW with SAP. We still recommend cleaning it up during the move. Most systems carry a lot of code that's no longer used, and every object you keep has to be adapted and tested.

How long does a conversion take?

For most companies, 12 to 24 months from planning to go-live. The main factors are system size, how much custom code there is, data quality, the number of integrations, and which path you take. We give you a firm plan after the readiness check.

How much downtime is there at go-live?

Usually a planned weekend. We rehearse the cutover several times on copies of your system, so the timing is measured, not guessed.

Do we have to upgrade every year?

No. With Private Edition, you decide when to move to a new release, within SAP's maintenance windows. We help you plan upgrades around your business calendar.

We're not on SAP today. Is RISE still an option?

Yes. Larger or more complex companies sometimes choose Private Edition for a new implementation, for example for industry coverage or deeper customisation. If you're a growing mid-sized company, GROW with SAP is often the better fit. We'll recommend one honestly.
