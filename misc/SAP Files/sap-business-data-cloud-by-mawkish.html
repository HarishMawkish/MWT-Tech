SAP Business Data Cloud · Technical guide

One data foundation for analytics, planning and AI.

SAP Business Data Cloud brings SAP Datasphere, SAP Analytics Cloud, SAP BW and SAP HANA Cloud together as one managed service, with SAP Databricks built in. SAP delivers your business data as ready-made data products that keep their meaning, and you combine them with the rest of your data. This page explains how it works underneath, layer by layer, and how we help you decide what you need.

01 · Overview

AI is only as good as the business context it gets.

A sales order number means little on its own. It becomes useful when it is linked to its customer, its deliveries, its invoices and the posting in your general ledger. Classic data platforms lose those links when they copy SAP tables, and teams spend months rebuilding them.

Business Data Cloud keeps them. SAP extracts the data from its applications, models it once, and delivers it as data products with the business meaning attached. Your reports, your planning, your data scientists and SAP's AI agents all work from that same governed foundation.

Turn data into outcomes

Reuse SAP's data products and ready-made applications across finance, supply chain and HR, instead of building each report from scratch.

Connect all your data

Combine SAP and non-SAP data, and share it with platforms such as Databricks, Snowflake and Google BigQuery without copying it.

Trustworthy AI

Joule and AI agents answer from governed, semantically rich data, so their answers are grounded in how your business actually runs.

02 · Architecture

How a Business Data Cloud landscape is put together.

You don't install anything. SAP runs the service, and you assemble your landscape from a cockpit: which applications you use, and which source systems feed them. SAP calls that grouping a formation.

Cockpit

Provisioned first

The control centre. Your administrator provisions it, then adds applications, connects sources and activates data products from here.

Formation

Apps plus sources

A formation links your Business Data Cloud applications to the SAP systems that feed them. Data products from those sources then become available to activate.

Public Edition

A certificate, not a connector

For SAP Cloud ERP Public Edition, trust is set up with a certificate exchange. No Cloud Connector, no on-premise components.

Existing tenants

Bring them along

Already run SAP Datasphere or SAP Analytics Cloud? They can join Business Data Cloud without a migration. It's mostly a contract change.

03 · Data products

From a table in SAP to a data product you can trust.

A data product is a curated, documented data set with an owner, a meaning and a defined way to access it. SAP builds its own data products from your SAP systems and keeps them current. Step through how one is made.

In SAP Cloud ERP

It starts with a CDS view

SAP's applications describe their data in CDS views: virtual data models that already know that a journal entry belongs to a company code and a ledger. These views are the source for SAP's data products, so the business meaning is there from the first step.

Federate or replicate?

Once a data product is installed in SAP Datasphere, you choose how each model reads it: query it where it lives, or take a local copy. Most landscapes use both.

How
Datasphere reads the data product where it lives. Nothing is copied.
Fresh
Always as current as the data product itself.
Best for
Exploring, lower volumes, and data you query occasionally.
Watch
Heavy, frequent queries can be slower and use more compute.

Governed from activation to use.

Data products are not free-for-all downloads. Activation loads the complete data set so every use case shares one version of it, and each team then requests access for a stated purpose.

01ActivateAn administrator activates a data product or a whole package. Everything is loaded: no field or row filters at this stage.
02RequestA data engineer finds it in the catalog and requests it for a space, with a purpose and an end date.
03ApproveAn approver accepts or rejects. The requester can't approve their own request.
04InstallThe product is installed into the target space in SAP Datasphere.
05RestrictData access controls in Datasphere decide who sees which rows.

Tip: data products carry personal-data tags. We check them before approval, so HR and customer data only reaches the spaces that should have it.

05 · SAP Datasphere

The knowledge core where your data model lives.

SAP Datasphere is where SAP and non-SAP data come together. Each team works in its own space, with its own storage, connections and users. We organise models in layers, so each step has one job and changes stay contained.

LAYER 1

Inbound

Data as it arrives: installed data products, replicated or federated tables, files.

LAYER 2

Harmonisation

Shared keys, currencies and master data. Data access controls are applied here.

LAYER 3

Propagation

Reusable business views, combined across sources, ready for any consumer.

LAYER 4

Reporting

Analytic models with measures and semantics for SAP Analytics Cloud and Excel.

Replication flows

Move data in

For SAP Cloud ERP, replication reads SAP's released extraction CDS views or uses the SQL service. Classic extractors and SLT don't apply to the cloud edition.

Transformation flows

Shape it

Graphical or SQL transformations, loaded in full or as deltas, and grouped into task chains that run on a schedule.

Semantics

Keep the meaning

Semantic types, associations and hierarchies tell every tool what an amount, a currency or a date really is.

Your own data products: a model you build in Datasphere can be published as a data product, for example a cleansed customer view, and shared with other teams or with Databricks like SAP's own.

07 · Open data ecosystem

Share SAP data with the platforms you already use.

Business Data Cloud is open by design. Data products are stored in an open table format and shared with Delta Sharing, so data platforms can read them without copies or extraction jobs. Results can come back as data products too, which closes the loop.

Questions we hear

Straight answers.

Does Business Data Cloud replace SAP Analytics Cloud or Datasphere?

No, it includes them. Business Data Cloud is the managed service that brings them together with data products, SAP Databricks and SAP HANA Cloud. If you already use Datasphere or Analytics Cloud, those tenants can join without a migration.

Do we need SAP Cloud Connector for SAP Cloud ERP Public Edition?

No. Public Edition connects with a certificate exchange. Other SAP deployments, such as on-premise systems, need the Cloud Connector.

Is our data copied?

SAP's data products are stored once in the Business Data Cloud data lake. From there they are shared, not copied, with Datasphere, SAP Databricks and partner platforms. You decide per model whether to take a local copy for performance.

Can we filter what gets activated?

Not at activation. A data product is activated in full so every use case shares one data set. You restrict access afterwards in Datasphere, by space and with data access controls.

Does it replace our SAP BW?

Not overnight. SAP BW can run alongside Business Data Cloud and connect to Datasphere, so you keep what works and move to data products at your own pace.

Which data products are available for SAP Cloud ERP Public Edition?

SAP adds data products with each release, and availability differs by edition. We check the current catalogue for your edition before we plan anything.

How is it licensed?

By capacity: you buy capacity units and the services consume them. Intelligent applications are added on top. We estimate capacity from your data volumes and use cases, and check it against your SAP contract.

We've just gone live on SAP Cloud ERP. Is it too early?

Usually, yes. Get comfortable with the built-in reporting first. Plan Business Data Cloud when you need to combine systems, plan, or use AI across your data.
