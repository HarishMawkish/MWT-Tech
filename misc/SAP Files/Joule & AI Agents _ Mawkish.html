Joule & AI Agents

Ask SAP. Then let it do the work.

Joule is SAP's AI assistant, built into SAP Cloud ERP. Ask it a question in plain English and it finds the answer in your live data. Ask it to act and it opens the right app with the fields filled in. SAP's AI agents go further: they take on whole tasks, such as clearing open items or fixing blocked orders, and hand you only the decisions. We help you put both to work, safely.

You stay in charge

You decide how much it does on its own.

An agent doesn't have to act alone from day one. SAP lets you set how much each assistant can do without asking. We start every agent at the safest level, measure how often its proposals are right, and only give it more freedom when the numbers support it.

Level 1

Suggest

The agent finds the work and proposes what to do. A person reviews and decides every case.

Where every agent starts
Level 2

Prepare

The agent prepares the documents. A person approves them in one click, or sends them back.

After proposals prove reliable
Level 3

Act and report

The agent acts within rules you set, such as a value limit, and reports what it did. Exceptions still come to a person.

Questions we hear

Straight answers.

Is Joule included in SAP Cloud ERP?

Joule's basic features are included. Assistants, agents and some advanced features are premium AI, licensed separately and charged by use. We tell you which is which for each use case.

Can an agent do something it shouldn't?

Agents work within the roles and authorisations of the user they act for, and within the autonomy level, limits and approvals you set. Their actions are recorded, so you can always see what was done and why.

Does Joule understand our company's terms?

Joule works on your live SAP data, so it knows your customers, products and documents. Your own terms, such as product nicknames, can be taught through custom skills and grounding in your own documents.

Can agents work with our non-SAP systems?

Yes. Agents built in Joule Studio can connect to other systems and to other companies' agents through open standards. We check what each system supports.

Where should we start?

With one high-volume task that annoys your team, such as clearing open items or resolving blocked orders. Prove it there, then expand.
