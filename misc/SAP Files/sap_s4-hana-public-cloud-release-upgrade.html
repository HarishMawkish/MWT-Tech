Release & Upgrade Management

A new SAP release every six months. No surprises on Monday.

SAP upgrades SAP Cloud ERP twice a year, on SAP's schedule, for every customer at once. You can't skip a release, and you wouldn't want to: each one brings new features and AI. But each one also changes screens, roles and processes your people rely on. We plan every upgrade, test what matters to your business, and switch on the new features worth having.

How SAP releases work

Two big releases a year, and smaller ones in between.

SAP Cloud ERP gets a major release every February and every August. Between them, SAP ships smaller feature deliveries and regular fixes. Everyone runs the same version, which is why SAP can move this fast, and why nobody falls years behind.

  1. February: major release (for example 2602).
  2. Spring: feature deliveries 2602.1 and 2602.2.
  3. August: major release (2608).
  4. Autumn: feature deliveries 2608.1 and 2608.2.
  5. All year: regular fixes.

Our upgrade plan

Six steps, every release, the same way.

SAP gives about six weeks' notice. It upgrades your test system first, and your development and production systems about three weeks later. Those three weeks are the window to find anything that would affect your business, so we plan the work before it opens.

Plan

Agree dates, testers and the freeze with you. Clear open changes so nothing is half-moved.

Before the freeze

Watch and adopt

Know before it happens

Only the changes that touch your business.

A major release has hundreds of changes across every industry and country. Most of them don't affect you. SAP's release assessment tool compares the release with the processes you actually use, and lists the changes, retired apps and new features that matter to you.

We go through that list with you, decide what needs testing or training, and track every item as a task until it's done. Role changes get special attention: after each upgrade, SAP's role catalogues change, and someone has to update your roles so people can still do their jobs.

Test what you use

Machines run the routine checks. People check what matters.

SAP Cloud ERP includes a test automation tool at no extra cost. We build automated tests for your main processes during implementation and keep them current, so every upgrade starts with a full regression run in hours rather than days. SAP also runs its own post-upgrade tests if you allow it.

The other half of an upgrade

New features you've already paid for.

Every release brings new features and AI capabilities, and many of them are switched off until you choose to use them. Most companies never look. We go through each release with you, show the useful features in your test system, and switch on the ones you want.

Some features can't be switched off again once they're on, so we test them first and agree the change with the people it affects.

What catches teams out

Six things we plan for every time.

None of these is hard on its own. Together, they're why some upgrades go quietly and others cause a difficult first Monday.

Transports freeze

Moves between systems stop a few days before the upgrade. Changes still in progress have to wait or be finished early.

Extensions out of step

Changes built on the old release may not move into an upgraded system, so we time them carefully.

Roles change

SAP's role catalogues change each release. Someone has to update your roles before people log in.

Content updates

New configuration content arrives in development first. Moving it on is your call, so we schedule it.

One-way switches

Some new features can't be turned off again. We test them before anyone switches them on.

Month-end clashes

When an upgrade lands near a close, we plan cover and extra checks around it.

Questions we hear

Straight answers.

Can we delay or skip an upgrade?

No. SAP Cloud ERP is one version for all customers, and SAP upgrades everyone on a published schedule. That's what keeps you current. The way to reduce risk is good testing, not delay.

Will the system be down during the upgrade?

Production is upgraded over a planned weekend window that SAP announces in advance. We tell your users when it is and plan around your critical dates.

How much of our team's time does an upgrade take?

With automated tests in place, mostly a few days of key-user checks during the test window, plus a short briefing on what's changed. Without automation, it's much more, which is why we build the tests early.

We didn't build automated tests during our implementation. Is it too late?

No. We start with your most important processes, such as order to cash, procure to pay and the month-end close, and add more each release.

Are new features switched on automatically?

Improvements to processes you already use arrive with the upgrade. Many new features stay off until you choose them, and new processes or countries are always your decision.

Is this included in your support service?

Yes, upgrade testing is part of our Managed and Extended support plans. We also offer it on its own for customers who support themselves.
