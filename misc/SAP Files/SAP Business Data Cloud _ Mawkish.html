SAP Business Data Cloud

Your business data, ready for analytics and AI.

Most companies still export SAP data to spreadsheets, or keep a data warehouse that breaks at every upgrade. SAP Business Data Cloud takes a different approach: SAP delivers your ERP data as ready-made data products that keep their business meaning and stay current, and you combine them with data from your other systems for reporting, planning and AI. We help you decide whether you need it, and set it up when you do.

Why it's different

Stop rebuilding SAP data. Use it as SAP delivers it.

The old way was to copy SAP tables into a warehouse and rebuild the business meaning yourself: what a posting is, how a sales order links to its delivery. It takes months, and every SAP release can break it. With Business Data Cloud, SAP does that work and keeps it up to date.

How it fits together

One data foundation, many uses.

Business Data Cloud brings SAP's data tools together. Data comes in from SAP Cloud ERP and your other SAP and non-SAP systems, is combined in one place, and is used for reporting, planning, data science and AI.

  1. Sources: SAP Cloud ERP, other SAP cloud apps and non-SAP systems.
  2. Data products: SAP-managed, plus your own.
  3. SAP Datasphere: combines and models the data.
  4. Uses: SAP Analytics Cloud for reports and planning, Databricks or Snowflake for data science, Joule and agents for AI.

An honest answer

Do you need Business Data Cloud?

Not every company does, at least not on day one. SAP Cloud ERP already includes strong real-time reporting for day-to-day operations. Business Data Cloud earns its place when you need to look across systems, companies or years, or want to plan and use AI on that data.

Your situationWhat usually fits
One company on SAP Cloud ERP, operational reportingThe analytics built into SAP Cloud ERP
Budgets and forecasts, a few dashboards for managementSAP Analytics Cloud on top of SAP Cloud ERP
Several systems or companies, group-wide analyticsBusiness Data Cloud with Datasphere
Data science, custom AI, or AI agents across your dataBusiness Data Cloud with data products
An existing SAP BW you want to moderniseBusiness Data Cloud, bringing BW along

Business Data Cloud is licensed by capacity, with intelligent applications on top. We size it for your data and use cases and check the terms in your SAP contract.

Questions we hear

Straight answers.

Does Business Data Cloud replace SAP Analytics Cloud?

No. SAP Analytics Cloud is part of Business Data Cloud. It's where people see dashboards and do planning. Business Data Cloud adds the data foundation underneath it.

Does it replace our SAP BW?

Not overnight. Business Data Cloud can include SAP BW, so you can keep what works and move to data products at your own pace.

Can we use it with Microsoft, Databricks or Snowflake?

Yes. SAP Databricks and SAP Snowflake are part of the offering, and SAP shares data with other platforms without copying it where possible. We check what fits your existing tools.

Which data products are available for SAP Cloud ERP?

SAP adds data products with each release, starting with finance, procurement and supply chain. We check the current list for your edition before we plan.

We've just gone live. Is it too early?

Usually, yes. Get comfortable with SAP Cloud ERP's built-in reporting first. Plan Business Data Cloud when you need to combine systems, plan or use AI across your data.
