What's included

Some AI comes with your subscription. Some you pay for as you use it.

SAP splits its AI into two groups. Base AI is included in your SAP Cloud ERP subscription. Premium AI, such as assistants, agents and some document processing, is licensed separately and charged by use. We tell you which group each use case falls into before you start, so there are no surprises on your bill.

How we work

Start small, measure, then scale.

AI projects fail when they start with the technology. Ours start with a list of tasks that take your people the most time, and a number for each one: hours spent, errors made, days lost. We pick two or three where SAP's AI fits, try them in your test system with your real data, and only move to production when the numbers prove it.

After go-live we keep measuring, so you can see what AI saves you every month, and decide where to go next.

Questions we hear

Straight answers.

Do we have to pay extra for AI?

Not for everything. Many AI features and Joule's basic features are included in SAP Cloud ERP. Assistants, agents and some document processing are premium and charged by use. We tell you which is which before you start.

Is our data used to train public AI models?

SAP's AI runs under your SAP contract and SAP's AI ethics and data protection commitments. We walk you through what those terms say for your subscription before you switch anything on.

Do we need a data science team?

No. Most of SAP's AI is built into the apps and works on your data as it is. You need people who know the process and can judge the results, which you already have.

We're still implementing SAP. Is it too early for AI?

No, it's the best time. Some AI features can be part of your design from the start, such as reading sales orders or payment advices, so you never build the manual process at all.

Does AI work with Sri Lankan documents and formats?

SAP's document reading handles many languages and layouts. We test it on your real documents first, including local bank advices, before we rely on it.
