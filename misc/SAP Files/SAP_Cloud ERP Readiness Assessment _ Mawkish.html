Questions we hear

Straight answers.

Isn't this just a sales exercise?

No. It's a fixed-fee piece of work with a written result you own. If SAP Cloud ERP isn't the right fit, or the timing is wrong, the report says so. Our reputation depends on recommending only projects that succeed.

What does it cost?

A fixed fee, agreed upfront, based on the number of departments and entities in scope. If you go ahead with Mawkish for the implementation, we credit the fee against the project.

What is SAP's Digital Discovery Assessment?

It's the structured questionnaire SAP uses to scope SAP Cloud ERP. It covers your industry, countries, processes, integrations and special requirements, and shows whether Public or Private Edition fits. We complete it with you during the workshops, and you receive the results as part of the report.

We're on SAP ECC. Is this different for us?

Yes. We add SAP's technical Readiness Check on your system. It shows how much custom code you really use, which changes affect you, and whether a conversion or a fresh start makes more sense.

Can you assess several companies in our group?

Yes. For groups, we look at how head office and the subsidiaries fit together. That often leads to a two-tier recommendation: RISE at the centre and GROW in the subsidiaries. The timeline grows with the number of entities.

What happens after the readout?

You keep everything. Many customers take the report to their board, then use the roadmap and licence sizing to request formal proposals. The fit map becomes the starting point for the project's workshops, so none of the work is wasted.
