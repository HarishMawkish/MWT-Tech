Our vision

To be the partner that makes enterprise technology simple, fast and intelligent for every business we serve.

Large companies and fast-growing ones come to us to run their finance, supply chain, sales and service on world-class platforms. Our job is to get them there with less effort and less risk than they expect.

We do that with small, senior-led teams that know the industry, follow the standard, and let AI do the repetitive work. That leaves people free for the parts that need judgement: understanding the business, designing the solution and earning the customer's trust.

01

Use AI for everything first

Before you start any task, ask how AI can do it faster or better. Drafting, analysis, testing, documentation, code: AI goes first, and you make it right.

02

Win as one team

No one owns a problem alone. Functional, technical and project people sit together, share what they learn and cover for each other when it matters.

03

The client's success is our success

We measure ourselves by what the client can do after go-live, not by hours or documents. If something is not right, we say so early and fix it.

04

Standard first, simple always

We follow the platform standard and keep designs clean, so customers can upgrade with ease. Clever is good. Simple is better.

05

Learn every week

The platforms change every quarter. So do we. Everyone has time to learn, certify and share what they found with the team.

06

Respect and openness

Ask questions, challenge ideas and give credit. Everyone's voice counts, from the newest intern to the most senior lead.

Who we look for

People who sit on the client's side of the table.

We hire people who are obsessed with client success. People who care about our clients' businesses as if they were their own, and who measure their work by what it changes for the client, not by what it took to deliver.

That means holding the line on quality, even when it is the harder path. If a design is not right, a test is not complete or a deadline puts quality at risk, we expect you to say so, push back inside Mawkish and fight for the right outcome. Internally, you speak for the client. Always.

  • Client success firstEvery decision starts with one question: is this right for the client?
  • Passion for the people we serveYou know their business, their pressures and their goals, and you care about them.
  • Uncompromising on qualityYou would rather raise a hard issue today than hand over something you are not proud of.
  • The courage to challengeYou challenge colleagues, leaders and plans, respectfully and with evidence, when the client's interest is at stake.
AI in how we work

We expect you to use AI on every task.

At Mawkish, AI is not a side project. It is how the work gets done. We give every team member access to AI tools, show them how to use them well, and expect them to look for a smarter way every day.

Our ground rules for AI
  • You are responsible for what AI produces. Check it before it reaches a customer.
  • Never put confidential customer data into a tool that is not approved for it.
  • Share prompts, agents and shortcuts that work, so the whole team gets faster.
ConsultantsDraft fit-to-standard notes, process designs and configuration guides with AI, then refine them with the customer.
DevelopersPair with AI coding assistants for ABAP, CAP, Apex and Lightning Web Components, and generate tests as you build.
Cloud engineersWrite infrastructure as code, runbooks and troubleshooting steps with AI, and review every change before it ships.
TestersTurn requirements into test cases and test data in minutes, then automate the runs.
Project officeUse AI to prepare status reports, minutes, risk logs and plans, so meetings are about decisions, not updates.
EveryoneUse Joule, Agentforce and other built-in AI so you can show customers what the platforms can really do.
