When something goes wrong

One place to call, and someone who knows your setup.

Your key users log an issue with us by portal, email or phone. We sort it by business impact, find the cause, and fix it. If the cause is a fault in SAP's software, we raise the case with SAP, give them what they need, and follow it through, so your team doesn't have to.

Most issues aren't bugs. They're master data, authorisations, or a step done in the wrong order. Each one we fix goes into a shared knowledge base, so the same question gets answered faster next time, often by your own key users.

Spot it before you do

We watch the parts that usually break.

In a cloud ERP, the system itself rarely fails. What fails is the work around it: an interface that stops, a job that ends with errors, a file the bank never receives. SAP Cloud ALM, which comes with your subscription, monitors these. We set it up for your processes and act on its alerts.

Users and performance

Twice a year

SAP upgrades your system. We make sure nothing breaks.

SAP Cloud ERP is upgraded twice a year on a fixed schedule, and you can't skip an upgrade. SAP gives about six weeks' notice and upgrades your test system first. That window is when we check everything: your processes, extensions, integrations, forms and roles.

  1. About 6 weeks ahead: notice from SAP.
  2. Test system upgraded: impact analysis for your scope.
  3. Regression testing: automated tests plus key-user checks.
  4. About 3 weeks later: production upgraded.
  5. After the upgrade: new features reviewed and switched on.

Our Release & Upgrade Management service covers this in detail. Within application management, it's part of the service.

Questions we hear

Straight answers.

SAP already supports the cloud system. Why do we need you?

SAP supports its software and runs the service. It doesn't know how your company codes, approvals, integrations and reports are set up, and it doesn't make changes for you. Most issues after go-live are about exactly that, so that's what we cover.

Can you support a system another partner implemented?

Yes. We start with a short handover review of your setup, extensions, integrations and open issues, document what we find, and then take over. If we see risks, we tell you up front.

What hours do you cover?

Sri Lankan business hours as standard, with extended cover for critical dates such as month-end, year-end and SAP upgrade weekends. Round-the-clock cover for critical issues can be agreed where you need it.

Do unused hours roll over?

That depends on the plan. In most plans, unused hours can go to improvement work, so the budget still goes into your system.

Do you support SAP add-ons and integrations too?

Yes. We support the integrations, extensions and SAP Business AI Platform services around SAP Cloud ERP, as well as the connected systems we implemented, such as Salesforce.

Will we become dependent on you?

We aim for the opposite. Your key users get training and a knowledge base, everything is documented, and you own all of it.
