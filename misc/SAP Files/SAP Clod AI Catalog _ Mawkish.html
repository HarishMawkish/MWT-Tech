Reading the catalog

Four kinds of AI, from helpful to hands-off.

Each item in the catalog is one of these. The further right, the more work it takes off your people, and the more thought goes into rules and approvals.

AI-assisted feature

Built into an app

Helps a person do a task faster, such as reading a PDF into a sales order or explaining an error.

Joule

Ask and act

SAP's AI assistant answers questions from live data and starts tasks for you.

Assistant

Looks after an area

Runs the routine steps of a whole job, such as accounts receivable, within limits you set.

Agent

Does a task end to end

Carries out one specific task, such as clearing open items, and hands you only the exceptions.

Questions we hear

Straight answers.

Is this SAP's official catalog?

No. SAP publishes its own full catalog of AI features in the SAP Discovery Center, covering all SAP products. This is Mawkish's guide to the AI that matters for SAP Cloud ERP customers, written in plain language, with our view on where to start.

What does "Mawkish quick win" mean?

It's our recommendation: AI that most companies can switch on early, with little change to how people work and a result you can measure within weeks.

Can we use these if we're still implementing SAP?

Yes, and it's often the best time. Some, such as reading sales orders or payment advices, can be part of your design from day one.

Why does availability say "beta" or "early adopter"?

SAP releases some AI to a limited group of customers first. We can tell you whether joining makes sense for you.

How often is the catalog updated?

We review it after each SAP release and each quarterly AI update.
