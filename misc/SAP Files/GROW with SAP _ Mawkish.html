Your differences, protected

Questions we hear

Straight answers.

What's the difference between GROW with SAP and RISE with SAP?

Both put you on SAP's cloud ERP. GROW is for a fresh start on the standard cloud product, shared with other customers and upgraded by SAP twice a year. RISE gives you your own private instance, more room for deep custom code, and the option to convert an existing SAP ECC system. Most growing mid-sized companies and subsidiaries fit GROW. Large groups with complex customisation usually fit RISE.

Can we keep our current SAP ECC configuration?

No. GROW is always a fresh implementation, but you can bring your data with you. Migration tools are included, and many customers find it a good chance to clean up years of old data and workarounds.

How long does an implementation take?

For a mid-sized company with standard scope, a few months rather than years. The biggest factors are how many processes and countries are in scope, how much data you migrate, and how quickly your people can join the workshops. We give you a realistic plan after the readiness assessment.

Will upgrades disrupt our business?

They're designed not to. You get about six weeks' notice, the test system is upgraded about three weeks early, and the test automation tool is included. Extensions built the clean-core way carry over automatically. We can run regression testing for you each release.

What if we need something the standard system doesn't do?

First we check whether a standard process or a configuration setting covers it. If it doesn't, there are three upgrade-safe options: key-user changes, developer extensions, and side-by-side apps on SAP BTP. We'll recommend the simplest one that does the job.

Does it work for our country?

SAP provides local versions for around 60 countries, covering tax, legal reporting and language. Requirements in other countries can be added through localisation. We'll confirm exactly what applies to your entities during the assessment.
